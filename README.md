# Dr. Mark

The lab's sexiest germ fighter. Drop two-colour capsules into the beaker and line up
4 of a colour to wipe out the germs: Fizz, Glitch and Zap.

Made for you by Mark ♥

**Phone (turn it sideways):** drag left/right to move · tap to rotate · drag down to speed up · flick down to drop · tap HOLD to save a capsule

**Keyboard:** A/D move · Space rotate · E hold · S soft drop · W hard drop · P pause
(2 players: second player uses the arrows, Enter to rotate and Right Shift to hold)

**Game controllers:** d-pad/stick move · A rotate · B hold · down soft drop · up hard drop · START pause
